%%DIALECT%% Pet Management Protocols: Keep Animals Secure

Even the friendliest dog can bite when stressed by a crowd of strangers.

Follow our morning-of pet protocol to keep your animals secure, stress-free, and legally protected.

Your %%CITY%% property is about to be packed with strangers. There will be loud haggling, people carrying large boxes, strollers rolling up the driveway, and unfamiliar scents everywhere. This is an incredibly high-stress environment for a domestic pet.
 
Even the gentlest, most well-behaved family dog can lash out, bite, or bolt into traffic when overwhelmed by a sudden crowd. Managing pets during high-traffic home events is not just about keeping your animal calm; it is about shielding yourself from severe legal liability.
 
The Morning-Of Pet Protocol:
  • The Absolute Rule: Never let your dog or cat roam free in the yard or driveway during a %%DIALECT%%.
  • Secure Interior Confinement: Place your pets in a quiet, interior back bedroom or laundry room with their food, water, and a comfortable bed. Lock the door.
  • The “Runner” Risk: If your front door is going to be opening and closing as you bring out supplies, or if shoppers are allowed inside the garage, you must ensure your pet is physically isolated behind a closed interior door so they cannot slip past your legs and escape.
  • Off-Site Alternatives: The absolute safest option for high-anxiety dogs is to remove them from the environment entirely. Book a day at a local doggy daycare, or ask a trusted friend or family member to pet-sit until the sale concludes.
 
If a dog bites a shopper on your property, your homeowner’s insurance will be heavily scrutinized, and you may face mandatory municipal quarantine protocols.
 

Protect your profits, your liability, and your pet by locking them safely away before the first buyer arrives.
