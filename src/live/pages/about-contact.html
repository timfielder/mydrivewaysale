About MyDrivewaySale

 

MyDrivewaySale.net was founded by Tim Fielder Enterprises, LLC to strip the stress out of the classic weekend garage sale. We compile messy, hard-to-find municipal laws, street sign rules, and organization tactics into clear, 10-step blueprints for local neighborhoods. Our goal is to help everyday families organize, price, and survive their neighborhood events efficiently.
