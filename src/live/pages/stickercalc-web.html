StickerCalc Lite: a free checkout for color-sticker sales

StickerCalc Lite is the free browser tool on this page. You give each sticker color a price and a short label, tap those colors as a shopper hands you items, and the web engine shows the total due. You then enter the cash they gave you, see the change to give, and tap PAID & COMMIT to add that sale to your session total.

It runs in your mobile or desktop browser, in the window below, and you do not have to install anything to try it. The phone app is a separate product, sold on the App Store and Google Play, if you want a native copy on your phone. This page describes the web tool you can launch right here.

How to use the web tool

  1. Build your sticker board. Under YOUR STICKERS, add a sticker for each color you actually put on the merchandise. Use Select Color, type a label (the tool itself uses examples like "Grab Bags"), and set the dollar value to match the physical sticker. You can add another sticker, edit the sticker layout, and save the layout so the board matches your tables.
  2. Tap to build the cart. When a shopper walks up, tap the color for each item. The current cart and the total due update as you tap. If you tap one extra, you can remove an item or clear the cart and the change field.
  3. Take the cash and commit. Enter the amount in Cash Given by Customer. The tool shows Change to Give. When the numbers look right, tap PAID & COMMIT. That sale is added to SESSION TOTAL, which is a running total of what you have sold in this session.

If you switch sticker packs mid-sale, the tool warns you that changing to a new physical sticker pack will clear the current active cart. Read that prompt before you confirm. Also, if you leave the page or exit while sales are active, the session total can clear. Keep the tab open while the sale is running.

Example pricing math

Suppose you set three colors to match your table: red is "Dishes" at $5.00, blue is "Books" at $1.00, and yellow is "Tools" at $10.00. A shopper hands you 2 red, 4 blue, and 1 yellow.

Total due:

  • 2 red dishes: 2 × $5.00 = $10.00
  • 4 blue books: 4 × $1.00 = $4.00
  • 1 yellow tool: 1 × $10.00 = $10.00
  • Total due = $24.00

If they hand you $30.00, change to give is $30.00 minus $24.00, which is $6.00. After you tap PAID & COMMIT, that $24.00 is added to the session total. The next shopper starts a new cart, and the session total keeps the running take for the day.

Do a practice cart like that before you open, the same way you would test a calculator. If the total due does not match your head math, a color value is wrong, and you want to catch it before the first real line.
