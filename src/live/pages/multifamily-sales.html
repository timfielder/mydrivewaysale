The Local Garage Sale Multi-Family Guide: Block Sales & Joint Permits

Did you know that pooling your inventory with neighbors can triple your foot traffic, but instantly creates a logistical nightmare for tracking who gets paid what?

Learn how to navigate joint-permit requirements, consolidate your checkout station, and seamlessly split the profits without a calculator.

Hosting a massive multi-family garage sale in your state is the ultimate way to attract serious buyers. Shoppers and professional pickers are far more likely to stop for a massive, multi-driveway block event than a small single-family setup.

However, combining households introduces immediate operational challenges regarding city ordinances, shared liability, and accurate end-of-day payouts. If you do not have a system in place before the crowds arrive, you will spend hours arguing over a messy paper ledger trying to figure out whose item just sold.

Before combining your inventory with other families, you must establish strict operational rules to keep the event legal and profitable:

  • Verify Joint Permit Rules: Some municipalities require a special “Block Sale” permit if multiple driveways are participating, or they limit how many households can legally operate under a single residential permit. Check your local regulations to see if each participating house in your city needs to file individually.
  • The Color-Dot Payout System:
