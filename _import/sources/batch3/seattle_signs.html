AG 1006: Yard Signs in the Public Right-of-Way

Overview 

We manage where you can place signs in the public right-of-way. This guide helps you understand the rules for placing yard signs, including campaign signs, safely and legally. 

I Want to Learn More About: 

  • Determining Eligibility
  • Planning Your Sign Placement 
  • Application Process
  • Enforcement and Complaints
  • Additional Resources

Determining Eligibility


You can place a yard sign in the public right-of-way without a permit, as long as you follow City guidelines. You can place signs in the planting strip right in front of private property. However, you must get direct permission from the person who lives or works on that property before you place the sign. 

Planning Your Sign Placement 

Before you place your sign, you need to make sure it meets all size and safety rules. These rules keep our sidewalks and streets safe for everyone. 

Size and Height Limits 

  • The sign must be 8 square feet or smaller. 
  • Signs can be up to 36 inches tall, measured from the street or driveway grade. 
  • If you place the sign within 30 feet of an intersection, it can only be 24 inches tall so drivers can see clearly. 
  • You cannot push the support stakes more than one foot into the ground. 

Placement Rules 

  • Do not block sidewalks or pedestrian paths. 
  • Do not block the view of drivers, cyclists, or pedestrians. 
  • Never attach your signs to City poles, streetlights, or other City infrastructure. 

Application Process 

There is no formal application to submit through the Seattle Services Portal for standard yard signs. You can place your sign as soon as you confirm it meets all the size and placement rules outlined above. Ensure you have the occupant's permission before putting the sign in the ground. 

Enforcement and Complaints 

We monitor the public right-of-way to keep our streets safe for everyone. We may issue citations to candidates and ballot issue committees if their volunteers place signs illegally. The City can also remove illegal signs without giving notice. 

Community members have the right to remove illegally placed signs in front of their own homes or businesses. For your safety, please do not remove signs from road medians or traffic circles. 

Additional Resources 

If you have questions or want to register a complaint about an illegal sign, please contact us. We ask that you provide a specific location and photos if you are submitting a complaint.
